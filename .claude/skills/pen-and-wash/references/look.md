# Look

Answer each with the evidence named. Read the plate's sheet (the site writes `figures-out/<id>.png`) at full size.

1. Does every object read as the thing it is, at 1x and at 4x? Evidence: the sheet and a 4x crop.
2. Is there one light, from the upper left, on every object? Evidence: name any shadow that falls the other way.
3. Does any hatch line leave its shadow, or cross in front of an object? Evidence: the 4x crop of the busiest shadow.
4. Do the light and dark plates show the same room, with no glow? Evidence: the two halves of the sheet side by side.
5. On a tiled plate, does the join disappear, and does a side unit show less than half of itself on the page? Evidence: the page at its widest view.
6. Is the signal only where something glows or marks? Evidence: list every orange mark.
7. Is every shadow a gradient: closer, finer lines at its dark edge, opening and shortening towards the light, and no crossed mid tone? Evidence: a 4x crop of the deepest shadow and of a lit edge.
8. In a picture, does every object read as the real thing, with its parts and its depth (a top, a front, a side)? Name one object that is texture without shape. Evidence: the picture at 4x.
9. Is the room ruled and the picture free? Evidence: a 4x crop where a picture meets its frame.
10. Is anything over-hatched: a hatch on a face that already has its shadow, lines far apart, or hatching beside or behind words? Evidence: list each hatched area and why it is in shadow.
