# Look

1. At 4x, is there any line that reads as ink rather than pencil? Evidence: a 4x crop of the busiest edge.
2. Does the distance sit back in both themes? Evidence: the far and near bands of the light and dark sheets.
3. Does every edge come from darker tone, the way a real pencil darkens? Evidence: name any edge made by a line.
4. Is there paper grain in the mid tones, and the full range from light to dark? Evidence: the G3 and G4 lines.
