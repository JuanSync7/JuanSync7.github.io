## Tone, not lines

- **R-BAKE-5** (eval: deck case only (no eval yet)) Draw a picture in graphite: depth from value, blended, with no hard line. Make an edge by darker tone, the way a real pencil darkens, never by a pressure line; a crisp stroke only where it shows the third dimension. *Why:* Hard lines stand out when zoomed in, and a pressure line drawn on top does not read as pencil. Reject when: an unfiltered stroke inside a picture, or sharp steps over the limit at 3x
- **R-BAKE-6** (eval: deck case only (no eval yet)) Separate near, middle and far by their distance from the paper colour, not by lightness, so the depth holds on dark paper too. *Why:* On dark paper the distance reads darker, not lighter; a lightness test passes light mode and fails dark. Reject when: a far band no closer to the paper colour than the near band
