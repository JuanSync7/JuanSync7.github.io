# Inspect the room

Read actual renders, not a description of the code. Record concerns with the seed,
renderer, projection and image path. A candidate reviewed by an agent is not a
human-approved exemplar.

1. **Purpose:** Without its caption, can the room be recognized by its furniture,
   architecture and activity zones rather than only one prop?
2. **Scale:** Do table, chair, monitor, door, cabinets and windows share a human scale?
   Does the room have usable floor space around its primary furniture?
3. **Arrangement:** Can a person enter, reach the work/seating zone and approach
   storage? Are seats aligned with their activity and furniture facing into the room?
4. **Completeness:** Are primary use, secondary use, storage and lighting represented?
   Does each added object contribute, and does the room retain breathing space?
5. **Architecture:** Are the wall finish, floor pattern, window covering, door and
   textiles plausible for this theme? Are frames, trim, recesses and hardware visible?
6. **Contact:** Do feet meet floor or a declared textile support? Are distinct rugs
   separated, and are distant edges correctly hidden by nearer faces? Do books and boxes sit
   on boards, do wall cases have backs/sides, and do projecting lights visibly connect to the room?
7. **Light:** Are pools and shadows consistent with windows and fixtures? Do dark
   materials retain detail, white surfaces retain highlights and metal retain sheen?
8. **Views:** Do eye level, both orbit ends and true isometric hold the room's important
   furniture? Are near-wall cutaways and hidden fixtures coherent in both renderers?
9. **Modes and sizes:** Does monochrome retain structure? Is colour coordinated rather
   than arbitrary? Do materials stay quiet at phone size and still at rest?
   Does the gallery open in colour, and do seed previews follow the selected appearance?

Required evidence: targeted figure sheet; twelve-seed sheet; all nine colour and
monochrome rooms; WebGL/fallback comparison; 0°, 90°, 135° and 180° camera captures;
mobile controls/width checks; geometry/program/material reports and real command exits.
