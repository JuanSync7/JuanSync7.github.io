# Look

Read each sheet; answer each question with what the sheet shows (the view and where).

## Wire and solid (stages 2 and 3)

- Does the silhouette read as the object from the side, the front and above, without labels?
- Are the proportions those of the real object at the chosen scale (length, width, height,
  wheelbase or storeys)? Name any that are off.
- Is every part that makes it recognisable there (for a car: wings, nose, sidepods, halo,
  airbox, wheels outboard)?

## LEGO (stage 5)

- Through all eight angles and the top and low views: is any piece inside another, or hanging
  in the air? (The check says no; the sheet is where a wrong check would show.)
- Does every slanted or curved surface use a slope, curve, wedge or round, not stepped bricks?
- Do the studs, tiles and grilles fall where a builder would put them?

## Figure (stage 6)

- From the first frame, is everything LEGO pieces on a LEGO base?
- Does each piece arrive by dropping onto studs, in an order a builder would follow?
- Does the teardown lift pieces off while the base stays?
- Does the model stay in frame through the whole loop, on desktop and phone?
- Under reduced motion, is the finished model shown?
