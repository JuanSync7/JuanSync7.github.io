# Look at the rendered icon

Read the actual proof, not just source coordinates. Preserve a proof hash and
answer each question with a visible feature and a judgment:

- **Recognition:** Does the silhouette express this slide's intended noun or metaphor?
- **View:** Do visible/hidden parts agree with the selected view? Does depth stay coherent?
- **Alignment:** Do connected parts meet? Are intentional gaps preserved? Do supports and repeated parts align?
- **Accent intent:** Name the component or functional set. Why does it express the brief? Is it the tick rather than a tick arm, the flame rather than a fin, the core rather than a square edge?
- **Accent completeness:** Are all visible members of that unit colored, with no unrelated parts? For equivalent dots, are all members of the selected role included?
- **Neutral/whole:** If there is no useful internal unit, is no accent the right choice? If the whole glyph is colored, does the recorded rationale justify that choice?
- **Small size:** Do the configured small and large proofs remain clear in every treatment?

A concern remains a concern until the changed proof is inspected. A fingerprint
attests which revision was reviewed; it does not authenticate the reviewer or
prove visual judgment happened. The author may self-review, but an independent
review claim requires a distinct reviewer and preserved evidence.

The review must bind `accentIntent` to the exact specification, including mode, part, rationale and basis. A structural pass only proves faithful rendering of that choice. It does not prove the choice is meaningful. User-directed cases have maintained regression expectations; unprovided choices remain author proposals until reviewed.
