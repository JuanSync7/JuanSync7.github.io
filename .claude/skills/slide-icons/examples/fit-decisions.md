# Fit decisions

**Reuse: generic bathtub.** The pinned Lucide `bath` silhouette includes a tub,
rim, two supports and a shower fixture. The generic facilities brief permits that
fixture. Keep the geometry, accent the complete straight rim and inspect its size proof.

**Create: freestanding bathtub with no shower.** The closest source includes fixed
plumbing and misses the brief. An original side view shows a rim, vessel and two
supports; the interior drain and rear supports remain concealed. Source and
constraints: `astro/vendor/slide-icons/candidates/freestanding-bathtub.json`.

**Reject:** copying the generic bath and calling its fixture-free fit “approved”
without inspecting it. **Accept:** recording the mismatch and creating a source
that communicates the intended object. These examples calibrate FIT and VIEW;
they do not require future subjects to be household objects.
