# Look

Read each drawing sheet and answer every question with what the sheet shows: the view, and its
grid reference where that helps.

## The views

- Read the projection symbol first. Is the end elevation to the right of the side elevation, and
  the plan below it?
- Does each view line up with its neighbour: the plan exactly as long as the side elevation and
  under it, the end elevation exactly as tall and beside it?
- Does the isometric view read as the object with no labels?
- Does every smooth part have its outline in every view, and is there no outline across a flat
  face (R-DRW-16)?
- Is every hidden edge dashed, and only where something really hides it? Is any edge missing?
- Does the chain line sit on the plane of symmetry in the plan and the end elevation?

## The numbers

- Is every size written once, in mm, on its own line as ISO 129-1 does it (R-DRW-12), with no table?
- Is a round part given once by its diameter, not as a length in each view (R-DRW-19)?
- Does the section cut every element cleanly, and do its profiles match the model?
- Do the overall dimensions match the research at the stated scale? Name the source for each.
- Is every scale a standard one, and does each view not at the sheet's scale say its own (R-DRW-17)?

## The set

- On the general arrangement: does every item have a balloon, clear of every part, its leader
  ending on the item it names, no two leaders crossing (R-DRW-20)?
- Does every balloon's number match a row of the parts list, and every row a drawing number
  that exists (R-DRW-18)?
- Does each drawing's title block give the same number, revision and document type as its row?

## The sheet

- Does any word touch a line or another word?
- Is the title block complete (ISO 7200), with the approval left for a person (R-DRW-10)?
- Do the notes say where the sizes came from, and whether a photograph was traced (R-DRW-2)?
- Is the sheet in ink only (R-DRW-9)?
