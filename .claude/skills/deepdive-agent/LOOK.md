# Inspect the drawing

Record the public seed, chain/era, level or time, source fingerprint, viewport and
artifact path. Compare the same inputs before and after. At least one inspected
case must be the reported failure, and the set must cover both chains and all eras.
Do not turn an uncertain answer into a measured pass.

**Chips**

- Can each visible wire be followed between actual pins, pads, contacts or junctions?
- Do the die edge, pad ring, I/O cells, keep-outs and routing channels read separately?
- Do repeated cores, memory banks and cell rows have consistent pitch and hierarchy?
- Do analog pairs/arrays read as matched devices with shared structure, rather than
  independent decorative spirals or rectangles?
- At close range, are vias/contact cuts intentional and distinct from trace crossings?

**Places and rooms**

- Can a person enter from the street, reach a building entrance and enter each room?
- Do wall cuts, door leaves/swings, windows, stairs and landings agree with each other?
- Does furniture communicate room use while leaving circulation and openings clear?
- Do streets form connected blocks with setbacks and meaningful frontages?
- Does a water crossing read as a bridge; do parks and buildings stay on valid land?
- Do coast, river and nested contours describe one terrain surface?

**The dive**

- Does the lit opening select a complete thing at the current scale?
- Can the relationship between parent and child still be understood during transition?
- Are level handoffs position-continuous, with no sudden border flash or orphan line?
- Do the rest frame, a phone-width frame and reduced motion remain readable?
- Is detail revealed with scale, or does distant detail become noise and moiré?

End the review with concrete remaining faults or `none observed in these cases`.
A finite sample does not prove every possible seed is visually perfect.
