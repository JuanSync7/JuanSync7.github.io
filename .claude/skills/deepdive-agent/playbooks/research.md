# Reference before geometry

**Use when:** changing a physical convention, silhouette, layout family or scale.
**Keep:** exact source URL, what was inspected, the observed relationship, and the
rule that will emulate it.

1. Reproduce the current public seed and take a close-up before editing.
2. Inspect a primary reference: a die photograph/layout from its designer, a documented
   floor plan, a city plan or an authoritative topographic map. Search snippets and
   image filenames alone are insufficient.
3. Identify topology first: pin/terminal relationships, repeated units and channels;
   entrance/corridor/room relationships; or streets, parcels, water and relief.
4. Record which proportions/conventions transfer to a small hairline plan and which
   details would be misleading at this scale. Emulate relationships; do not bundle
   third-party images or trace protected assets without a reuse basis.
5. Add the reference and the resulting design decision to the relevant
   `astro/docs/deepdive-*-references.md` file. Give the decision a rejection case.

**Reject:** name-dropping a reference without looking, decorative detail without
physical purpose, or a claim of engineering sign-off from a visual study.
