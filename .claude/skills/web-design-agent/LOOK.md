# Visual review

## Rooms

Use the existing tabletop-room look questions for scale, usable zones, supports,
architecture, camera and recognisable objects. Add these lighting questions:

- Can I name the main source from the visible room and the shadows it produces?
- Does one direction model form while weaker fill opens the shadows?
- Can I read wall corners, furniture fronts and table items in monochrome?
- Are paper, timber, metal, fabric and recessed areas separated in value/texture?
- Do cabinet doors, chair rails and wall trim remain readable on their own, rather
  than bright plates making the whole-image histogram look healthy? Check wall
  feet/corners for gentle contact falloff and reject screen-fixed vignettes.
- Does the table remain the primary activity without enlarging it to fill the room?
- Do same-theme seeds vary coordinated colour, construction and secondary layout?
- Do evening and low-light rooms still have usable worktops and readable routes?
- Are halos small, attached to emitters and hidden by opaque objects?
- Does haze add gentle depth only where intended, without hiding objects?
- Do full colour → monochrome → full colour preserve camera, geometry and materials?
- At eye 0° and 180°, do the ceiling, floor and sources still belong to one room?
- Is the phone composition useful, and is the simpler fallback coherent?

Read the four within-theme sheets, both nine-theme colour/mono galleries and both
endpoint/ceiling sheets. Record actual source revision and seeds. Use before/after
captures with identical camera and size for claims about improvement. Do not infer
visual quality from a passing histogram or call output inspected before opening it.

## Voxel atmosphere

Read the before/after comparison and the numbered atmosphere sheet, then both template
seed sheets and phone captures. Record image hashes and uncertain judgments.

- Do evening light and moonlight describe roof slopes, tree canopies and terrain relief?
- Are cast shadows attached to the objects that cause them? Can shadowed detail still be read?
- Do windows/fire glow from real geometry, with local illumination rather than a global wash?
- Does rain look wet and overcast; does snow have cool, soft light; does mist separate depths?
- Is weather visible at whole-world and phone scale without covering the scene in noise?
- Do rain/snow stop above trees/roofs, and does weather off retain local fire and windows?
- Are night and cave scenes readable? Does intentional close zoom preserve useful context?
- Do reduced motion and fallback retain the composition? The fallback has directional face
  shading, fog tone and weather, but no GPU mapped shadow; state that limit honestly.

Pixel differences establish that an effect exists. They do not establish tasteful colour,
believable intensity or readability. Do not replace these questions with pass flags.
