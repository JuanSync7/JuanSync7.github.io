# Look at the evidence

Read the before/after comparison and the numbered atmosphere sheet, then both template
seed sheets and phone captures. Record image hashes and uncertain judgments.

- Do evening light and moonlight describe roof slopes, tree canopies and terrain relief?
- Are cast shadows attached to the objects that cause them? Can shadowed detail still be read?
- Do windows/fire glow from real geometry, with local illumination rather than a global wash?
- Does rain look wet and overcast; does snow have cool, soft light; does mist separate depths?
- Is weather visible at whole-world and phone scale without covering the scene in noise?
- Do rain/snow stop above trees/roofs, and does weather off retain local fire and windows?
- Are night and cave scenes readable? Does intentional close zoom preserve useful context?
- Do reduced motion and fallback retain the composition? The fallback has directional face
  shading, fog tone and weather, but no GPU mapped shadow; state that limit honestly.

Pixel differences establish that an effect exists. They do not establish tasteful colour,
believable intensity or readability. Do not replace these questions with pass flags.
