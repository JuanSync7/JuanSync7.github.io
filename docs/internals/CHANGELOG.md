# Changelog

## 2026-11-06

Slide icons adds 24 simple computing, systems, data and research marks for presentations. Transparent SVGs share a 64-unit grid and one stroke family, with dark/light ink and teal-accent/monochrome treatments. The Lab collection supports filtering, search, clipboard export and downloads of each icon or the complete 96-variant set. Build-time exports, independent ZIP checks and browser checks keep the assets and interface in step.

## 2026-11-05

The hardware atlas models now include populated boards, die floorplans, bonding fields, recessed sockets, serviceable server internals, mechanical fasteners, heat pipes and retained optics. Engineering detail and isolated-part inspection make those features readable at full size; isolated poses can be shared and exported. Depth dependencies prevent raised panels from being hidden by lower parts.

The Lab gains a hardware atlas: twelve assemblies with matching isometric and wireframe SVGs, named parts, camera presets, exploded views and a downloadable 24-symbol sprite. The SVG collection and seeded figure share one geometry source, the existing night tokens and figure clock. Geometry, export and browser checks cover the new experience.

## 2026-11-04

Lessons can be browsed by maintained category or by month, and filtered by category and multiple typed tags. The URL preserves selections. All historical prose remains unchanged; seven source-backed recent lessons are added. The vocabulary and public payloads come from the vault, with schema, whole-collection, sync and browser checks.
